Every prescription is checked by a registered pharmacist. Every appointment starts on time. And every price is matched if you find it lower nearby.

05 · Department by department

Every specialty, on the same street.

6 departments · One roof · One waiting room

Specialists who share a corridor — and your file

When your GP wants a second opinion, the cardiologist is twenty steps away, not twenty days away. Departments at Freewheel+ share notes, results and follow-ups, so care moves at the speed of a hallway conversation.

Everyday illness, infections, checkups and referrals — our GPs keep same-day slots open every morning, and walk-ins are seen before lunch whenever possible.

Vaccinations, growth checks and gentle sick-day care for ages 0–16, with evening slots so no child has to miss school — and no parent has to miss work.

ECG, echo and blood-pressure reviews done in-house the same visit, with results read before you leave — and a direct line to your GP's notes.

Exams, cleaning and fillings in a chair that faces the window, not the wall. Anxious patients get the first appointment of the day and as long as they need.

Routine screenings, contraception advice and antenatal support with female clinicians — private rooms, unhurried appointments, no judgment.

Blood panels, glucose, ECG and rapid tests processed in our on-site lab — most results back within hours and reviewed by your doctor, not just emailed.
